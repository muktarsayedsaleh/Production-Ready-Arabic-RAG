# Salary bands 2026

Document HR-CONF-01. Confidential: HR only. Effective 1 January 2026.

Monthly basic salary in SAR, before allowances. Housing allowance is 25% of basic and
transport allowance is 10% of basic for every band.

| Band | Example roles | Minimum | Midpoint | Maximum |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | Warehouse associate, driver | 4,500 | 5,500 | 6,500 |
| B2 | Customer support agent, accountant | 7,000 | 8,750 | 10,500 |
| B3 | Senior accountant, sales executive | 11,000 | 13,500 | 16,000 |
| B4 | Team lead, senior engineer | 16,500 | 20,000 | 23,500 |
| B5 | Department manager | 24,000 | 29,000 | 34,000 |

## Rules

- New hires start between the minimum and the midpoint of their band. Starting above the
  midpoint needs approval from the HR director.
- The annual increase pool for 2026 is 4% of total basic salaries. Employees above the
  maximum of their band receive their increase as a one-off payment instead.
- Promotion to the next band comes with an increase of at least 8%, or up to the new band's
  minimum if that is higher.

# Expense reimbursement policy

Document FIN-POL-07. Effective 15 February 2026. Owner: Finance, Rimal Distribution.

## Scope

This policy covers business expenses that employees pay themselves and claim back. Travel
allowances are covered by the travel policy (HR-POL-11), not here.

## What can be claimed

- Client meals, up to SAR 250 per person, with the names of the attendees on the claim.
- Taxis and ride-hailing for business trips inside the city.
- Mobile data top-ups while working away from the office, up to SAR 100 per month.
- Books and online courses approved in advance by the line manager.

Alcohol, traffic fines, personal entertainment and upgrades to business class are never
reimbursed.

## How to claim

Submit form EXP-7 in the finance portal within 30 days of the expense, with a scanned
receipt for every item over SAR 50. A tax invoice is required for any item over SAR 1,000.
Claims older than 90 days are rejected without review.

Each claim needs one approval from the line manager. Claims over SAR 5,000 also need
approval from the finance controller.

## Payment

Approved claims are paid with the next monthly payroll if approved before the 20th of the
month, otherwise with the payroll after that. Payment is made to the salary account only.

## Corporate cards

Employees with a corporate card must use it for business expenses instead of paying
themselves. Card statements are reconciled in the finance portal by the 5th of the
following month; an unreconciled statement suspends the card.
